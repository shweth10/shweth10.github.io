FAQ

Mileage & fuel log questions

Does LensReport track mileage and fuel?
Yes. Use the mileage template to log distance with a cents-per-km or per-mile rate as a real formula, and scan fuel and toll receipts straight into a fuel log. Totals recalculate live in Excel or Google Sheets.
Can my crew add entries?
Yes. On a paid plan you invite your team to a shared report. Each person logs mileage or snaps fuel receipts from the field, tagged to them, and you approve — no chasing paper at month-end.
Are the totals real formulas?
Yes. The claim amount (distance × rate) and fuel totals are written as working Excel formulas, so they recalculate when you edit a cell and open intact in Google Sheets and Numbers.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Personal data such as emails, phone numbers, card numbers and government IDs is scrubbed before the AI ever sees it, and images are processed under a zero-data-retention agreement.
What does it cost?
It's free to start — no card needed. The free tier includes a set number of scans and exports each month; team collaboration and higher limits are on the paid plans.
