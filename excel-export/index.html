Questions people ask

What is in the export?

Every expense in the ledger, one per row: date, merchant, description, category, amount, payment method and notes.

Can I export just one month?

The export covers the whole ledger; filter by date in Excel or Sheets once it is open.

Does it open in Google Sheets?

Yes. The .xlsx file opens in Excel, Apple Numbers and Google Sheets.

Is export free?

Exports need a subscription. Logging your three free expenses does not.

Can my accountant use it?

Yes. The columns are plain values, so an accountant or bookkeeper can sort, filter and total them however they like.
