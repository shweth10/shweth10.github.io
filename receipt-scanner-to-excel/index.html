FAQ

Questions about scanning receipts to Excel

Does LensReport export real Excel formulas?
Yes. Calculated columns — totals, tax, subtotals — are written as real working Excel formulas, not flattened numbers. Edit a cell and the spreadsheet recalculates, exactly like one you built by hand. When a receipt prints its own final total, that authoritative value is preserved instead.
Is my receipt data safe?
Yes. Personal data such as emails, phone numbers, card numbers and government IDs is scrubbed before the AI ever sees it, and scanned images are processed under a zero-data-retention agreement. LensReport doesn't sell data or run ads.
Does the Excel file open in Google Sheets?
Yes. The Excel (.xlsx) and CSV exports open directly in Google Sheets and Apple Numbers with the formulas intact — no conversion or import gymnastics.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Capture receipts offline and they queue on your device, then process automatically when you reconnect. Editing reports and exporting work offline too.
What does it cost?
It's free to start — no card needed. The free tier includes a set number of scans and exports each calendar month; paid plans unlock higher limits, team collaboration, custom columns and AI Insights.
