Questions people ask

Which iPhones does it support?

Any iPhone running iOS 15.5 or later. The app is on the App Store now.

Is there an Android version?

A Google Play version is in closed testing. The button on this site will go live when it is public.

Does it sync between devices?

Sign in with the same account and your ledger is there. A subscription keeps everything backed up in the cloud.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export your ledger to Excel or PDF from Settings; a subscription is required for exports.
