Questions people ask

Where do the numbers come from?

From your entries. If the plan says weekday coffees cost $58 a month, that is the sum of the coffees you logged. The AI writes the sentence; it does not make up the figure.

What if I have barely logged anything?

You get tips for the spend areas you choose and an honest note that there is not enough data yet, instead of a made-up plan.

Does it judge my spending?

No. The tips are about patterns, never about whether a purchase was a good idea.

Can it set the budget for me?

When a move suggests a cap, one tap opens the budget with that limit filled in.
