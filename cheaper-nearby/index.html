Questions people ask

Does it track my location?

No. You type the suburb or area you spend in, once. No GPS, nothing stored about where you are.

Are the prices real?

The places, ratings and distances are real and come from Google. Prices are estimates from the type of place and its price level, and the app says so on every result.

Why did nothing show up for my coffee?

When fewer than two well-rated places nearby are likely to be cheaper, LensReport says so rather than listing something it cannot stand behind.

Which expenses trigger it?

Coffee, takeaway, dining, groceries, fuel, pharmacy and personal care, when the amount is $5 or more.
